## Company Overview

Palantir Technologies Inc. (NYSE: PLTR) is a software company specializing in enterprise data integration, operational analytics, and artificial intelligence deployment. The company designs and deploys foundational software platforms:

* **Palantir Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP):** Allows enterprises to bind Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI agents to operational data ontologies.
* **Palantir Foundry:** Serves as the core operating system for enterprise data management across commercial clients.
* **Palantir Gotham:** Focuses on defense, intelligence, and government operational decision-making.
* **Palantir Apollo:** Delivers continuous software deployment across dynamic environments.

Palantir operates across two primary customer segments: **Commercial** and **Government**. Operatively, the business has successfully transitioned from high-touch human consultancy deployments toward a scalable, high-margin software platform model. Recent enterprise implementations include managing Nvidia’s multi-tiered 1.3 million-part supply chain and infrastructure alliances with partners such as Nebius [Web Search].
