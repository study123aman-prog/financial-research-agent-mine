## Company Overview

Amazon Inc. is a global technology hyperscaler operating across enterprise cloud infrastructure, digital retail, advertising, and subscription services. The company is organized into three principal segments: North America, International, and Amazon Web Services (AWS). 

* **Sector:** Consumer Discretionary / Information Technology Infrastructure
* **Key Products & Services:**
  * **AWS Cloud Infrastructure (IaaS/PaaS):** Compute (EC2), Storage (S3), Database primitives (Aurora, DynamoDB), Serverless architectures, and AI platform services (Bedrock, SageMaker).
  * **Proprietary Hardware & Silicon:** ARM-based Graviton CPUs, Trainium2 AI training chips, and Inferentia2 AI inference accelerators.
  * **Retail & Digital Subscriptions:** E-Commerce marketplaces, Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA), Amazon Prime subscription ecosystem, and digital advertising platforms.

## Risk Assessment

Evaluating regulatory filings, earnings trends, and industry disclosures yields five primary strategic and financial risks for Amazon:

1. **Capital Intensity & Yield Compression:** CapEx scaling—reflected in the $65.756B D&A expense for FY2025 (+24.55% YoY)—carries risks if enterprise AI adoption slows or fails to generate expected long-term cash flow yields.
2. **Competitive Market Share Erosion in Cloud:** Microsoft Azure (~23–25% market share) leverages existing enterprise software bundling (Office 365, Enterprise Agreements), while Google Cloud (~10–11% market share) competes aggressively on AI analytics, threatening AWS’s market leadership.
3. **Silicon Supply Chain & Technological Execution Risk:** AWS relies on its custom Graviton, Trainium2, and Inferentia2 silicon to lower compute unit costs. Fabrication delays, component bottlenecks, or architecture shifts away from proprietary chips could compress operating margins.
4. **Macroeconomic Enterprise Optimization:** Periodic enterprise cloud optimization cycles can impact spending velocity, as enterprise clients negotiate longer-term Savings Plans at discounted unit rates.
5. **Regulatory & Anti-Trust Scrutiny:** Amazon faces ongoing global regulatory oversight regarding third-party marketplace practices, ecosystem bundling, and platform self-preferencing.
