## Risk Assessment

1. **AI Infrastructure CapEx Drag on Margins**: Gross margins contracted 183 bps between FY24 and FY26 due to intensive capital outlays in AI data centers, specialized accelerators, and energy infrastructure [Source: SEC EDGAR]. Failure to sustain high-yield monetization on these expenditures could pressure cash flows.
2. **Hyperscale Cloud & AI Competition**: Rivalry from Alphabet (Google Cloud/Gemini) and Amazon (AWS/Bedrock) threatens cloud market share and pricing power, with competitors aggressively cutting prices or offering bundled hardware-software solutions [Source: Yahoo Finance, NewsAPI + TextBlob].
3. **OpenAI Strategic Dependency**: Microsoft’s reliance on its partnership with OpenAI for core LLM integrations poses model concentration and corporate governance risks if joint alignment or governance structures fracture [Source: NewsAPI + TextBlob].
4. **Antitrust & Regulatory Scrutiny**: Global regulators (FTC, EU European Commission) continue to scrutinize Microsoft’s market position in enterprise cloud bundling, security software defaults, and strategic AI partnerships [Source: SEC EDGAR].
5. **Enterprise IT Spending Sensitivity & Macro Volatility**: Enterprise seat-based SaaS expansion and cloud consumption models remain exposed to broader macroeconomic headwinds, foreign currency fluctuations, and corporate IT budget freezes [Source: SEC EDGAR].
