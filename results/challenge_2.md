## Company Overview

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, integrated with proprietary operating systems (iOS, macOS, iPadOS) and custom Apple Silicon architectures. 

* **Primary Product Segments:** iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Wearables, Home & Accessories.
* **Services Division:** High-margin ecosystem revenue comprising the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, Apple TV+, AppleCare, and advertising platforms.
* **Strategic Focus:** Scaling edge-AI capabilities ("Apple Intelligence") embedded natively across hardware lines while leveraging custom silicon to expand gross margins and reinforce user retention across an installed active device base exceeding 2 billion units.
