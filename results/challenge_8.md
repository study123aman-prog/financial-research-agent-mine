## Company Overview
NVIDIA Corporation operates in the Technology sector within the Semiconductor industry. Originally established as a pioneer in 3D graphics processing units (GPUs) for gaming, NVIDIA has transformed into a full-stack computing infrastructure provider powering enterprise artificial intelligence, high-performance computing (HPC), and cloud data centers. 

The company’s portfolio is built around compute architecture (Hopper, Blackwell, and successor platforms), integrated networking solutions (Mellanox InfiniBand and Ethernet systems), and the proprietary CUDA software architecture, which creates significant technological lock-in across software development ecosystems. Operating globally, NVIDIA supplies hardware platforms and enterprise software services to major hyperscale cloud providers, enterprise IT departments, research institutions, and automotive manufacturers.
